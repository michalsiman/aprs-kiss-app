APRS & Packet – privacy policy

Applies to the APRS & Packet app on Android, iOS, Linux, Windows and macOS. Last change: 23 November 2026.

In short

The author collects nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to the author. What you set up stays on your device.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your settings (callsign, position typed in, TNC connection, APRS-IS passcode, choices on the screens).
  • Messages, heard stations and the BBS / TAK data, so they are there after a restart.

They are stored only in the app's own folder on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Location

When you choose Settings › GPS › The phone's GPS, the app reads the phone's location while it is running (never in the background). It uses it for the map and – only if you turn transmitting or APRS-IS on – in your position report (beacon).

What goes out, and where

  • By radio: what you transmit (your callsign, position, messages, packet traffic) is sent on air and can be received by anyone. Transmitting is off until you switch it on and needs an amateur radio licence.
  • APRS-IS (only when you enable it in Settings): your callsign, position reports and messages go to the APRS-IS server you choose; APRS-IS is a public network and its data is shown on public sites such as aprs.fi.
  • Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org); like any web request it sees your IP address and which map area is loaded. The offline map needs no internet.
  • New-version check (builds from GitHub; it can be switched off in Settings): once a day the app asks api.github.com for the latest release; GitHub sees your IP address. The Google Play build has no such check.
  • Links you tap (aprs.to, GitHub, the author's site) open in your browser.

Those services have their own privacy policies; the app shares nothing with them beyond what is described here.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet – APRS-IS, map tiles, a TNC on the network.
  • Location – only for “The phone's GPS”, asked for when you choose it.
  • USB – a TNC on the phone's USB port; Android asks before the app may use the device.

Children

The app is meant for licensed radio amateurs, not for children.

Zásady ochrany osobních údajů (česky)

Stručně: autor nic nesbírá. Aplikace nemá účet, analytiku, reklamy ani sledování a autorovi nic neposílá. Nastavení, zprávy a slyšené stanice zůstávají jen v zařízení a s odinstalací zmizí.

Poloha: při volbě Nastavení › GPS › GPS telefonu čte aplikace polohu telefonu, jen když běží (nikdy na pozadí), pro mapu a – jen když zapneš vysílání nebo APRS-IS – pro hlášení polohy (maják).

Co odchází ven: rádiem to, co vysíláš (značka, poloha, zprávy, packet) – přijmout to může kdokoli; do APRS-IS (jen když ho zapneš) značka, poloha a zprávy – APRS-IS je veřejná síť zobrazovaná např. na aprs.fi; dlaždice mapy z OpenStreetMap (vidí IP adresu a oblast mapy); kontrola nové verze na api.github.com jednou denně (lze vypnout, verze z Google Play ji nemá).

Oprávnění: internet (APRS-IS, mapa, TNC v síti), poloha (jen pro GPS telefonu), USB (TNC na USB, Android se zeptá). Aplikace je určena radioamatérům s licencí, ne dětem.
